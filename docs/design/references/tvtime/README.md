# TV Time references

Screenshots of TV Time taken on Delasi's iPhone in July 2026, before the app shut down. They are layout references for Showlog. They are not shipped in the app. Posters in them belong to their owners. Do not copy TV Time's colours or logo.

- `01-watch-list.jpg` — Watch next list. One card per show: episode still, show name pill, `S01 | E08 +1`, episode title, tick button.
- `02-watch-list-grid.jpg` — the same list as a poster grid with a progress bar under each poster.
- `03-upcoming-recent.jpg` — Upcoming, scrolled up to recently aired episodes grouped by date, with ticks and a NEW badge.
- `04-upcoming-today-later.jpg` — Upcoming grouped Today, Friday, Later. Air time and network on the right. LATEST badge. "7 days" countdown for later episodes.
- `05-upcoming-grid.jpg` — Upcoming as a poster grid with day countdowns.
- `06-my-shows-grid.jpg` — all shows as a three-column poster grid with a coloured progress bar and a Filters button.
- `07-my-shows-sort-filter.jpg` — Sort by Last watched, Last added, Alphabetical. Filter by All, Watching, Haven't started, Watch later, Up to date, Finished, Stopped.
- `08-profile-stats.jpg` — profile with stats: TV time (8 months, 1 day, 12 hours) and episodes watched (9,629).
- `09-profile-shows.jpg` — profile shows row and favourites (favourites are not in Showlog).
- `10-show-about.jpg` — show header: full-width picture, name, seasons and network, progress bar, About and Episodes tabs.
- `11-show-episodes-seasons.jpg` — Episodes tab: Continue tracking carousel, then collapsible seasons with "10/24" and a season tick.
- `12-season-episodes.jpg` — an open season: episode still, `S04 | E01 (E73)`, title, tick.
- `13-season-unaired.jpg` — unaired episodes show a day countdown instead of a tick.
- `14-show-menu.jpg` — show menu: Watch later, Stop watching, Remove show (Customize, Favorite, Add to list, and Share are not in Showlog).
- `15-show-about-streaming.jpg` — About tab for a streaming show.
- `16-show-continue-tracking.jpg` — Continue tracking on a one-season show.
